# Role Map And Study Plan

## What matters

This role sits between customer intent and delivered product behavior. A strong System Development Engineer turns a broad feature idea into clear requirements, a workable architecture, a verification plan, and controlled change handling.

## How to read the job offer

The offer can be split into five interview categories:

1. Systems engineering thinking
2. Requirements and architecture
3. Power electronics and automotive domain knowledge
4. Verification, quality, and safety processes
5. Leadership, negotiation, and communication

## What the interviewer is likely testing

- Can you work through the full V-cycle instead of only one silo?
- Can you discuss technical tradeoffs, not just final decisions?
- Do you understand how requirements become tests?
- Can you work with hardware, software, and testing teams at the same time?
- Can you handle changes, supplier inputs, and customer pressure without losing traceability?

## Your study focus by priority

### High priority

- V-model and systems engineering lifecycle
- Requirements quality and traceability
- Verification and validation strategy
- FMEA, FTA, ASPICE, ISO 26262 basics
- Inverter and motor-drive fundamentals at interview depth

### Medium priority

- SysML, Simulink, Polarion, DOORS
- Sensors, actuators, and embedded software collaboration
- Customer communication and design review structure

### Useful bonus topics

- Supplier management in technical projects
- Change request triage
- Mentoring junior engineers
- Presenting to mixed technical and non-technical audiences

## A practical preparation method

For each topic, prepare three levels of answer:

1. A one-sentence definition
2. A five-sentence practical explanation
3. One real or realistic example with a tradeoff

Example:

- One sentence: Requirement traceability links customer need to system requirement, component implementation, and verification evidence.
- Practical explanation: Without traceability, teams cannot prove coverage, assess change impact, or explain gaps during reviews.
- Example: If inverter torque response timing changes, traceability helps identify which system requirements, software modules, and test cases must be updated.

## Practice task

Write a short self-introduction for this role in 8 to 10 lines. It should connect your background to systems engineering, cross-functional work, and product verification.
